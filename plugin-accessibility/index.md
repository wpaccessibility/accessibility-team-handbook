# WordPress Plugin accessibility

There are no plugin accessibility requirements in the WordPress plugin directory. This is because of the labor required to test plugins for accessibility. However, you are strongly encouraged to consider accessibility when creating or updating your plugins.

## Why is plugin accessibility important?

Accessibility is the key to making your plugin usable by people with disabilities. The accessibility of your user interface has an impact on what countries and organizations can legally use your plugin. 

Accessibility is required by many governments for any commercial site, and by even more for sites within governmentally supported institutions such as schools. The laws governing accessibility are variable, but most laws reference a set of guidelines called the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines](https://wpaccessibility.org/docs/topics/wcag/) (WCAG), authored by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

In the WP Accessibility Knowledge base you will find information about [legislation](https://wpaccessibility.org/docs/legislation/), [standards and best practices](https://wpaccessibility.org/docs/topics/).
